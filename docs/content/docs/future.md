With text-based gaming having entered niche status, the goal is to build on project's rich [history](./history.md) while adapting for the times.
Some things that we'll need to keep in mind as we build:

1. We must provide gameplay depth while looking for ways to make the experience more intuitive.
1. Fun factor should be prioritized over strict adherence to rule sets and technical readouts.
1. We should be open to re-thinking how things have worked for decades in service of the above points.
